# TELL Archetype PNG Cutouts: Visual Design System & Specification

> **Status**: Approved Direction (Tested & Verified with *The Sovereign Entrepreneur* & *The Techno-Cosmopolitan*)  
> **Target Format**: 3:4 Aspect Ratio (896 × 1200 px), Transparent PNG Cutouts with Alpha Channel  
> **Framing**: Three-Quarter (3/4) Composition (Hips/Waist to Head)  
> **Card Surface**: Obsidian Dark Glass (`#0E121B`) with Dynamic Theme-Colored Ambient Backlight

---

## 1. Core Design Philosophy

Political archetypes represent **philosophies, ideals, and worldviews—not specific demographics**. 

To make every archetype universally relatable to any user regardless of gender, age, or ethnicity, the visual assets adhere to four non-negotiable principles:

1. **Gender-Neutral / Demographic-Free Visages**:
   - Zero facial hair, zero cosmetics, zero gender-typed hairstyles.
   - Headpieces utilize **reflective aerodynamic dark-glass visors, deep monolithic hoods/cowls, sculpted minimalist helmets, or high-shadowed profiles**.
   - Any user receiving their result projects *themselves* onto the archetype.
2. **Three-Quarter (3/4) Framing (Hips/Torso to Head)**:
   - Full-body shots leave characters looking small due to empty space around shoes.
   - 3/4 framing fills the playing card with dramatic presence, giving prominence to the chest attire, silhouette drape, and the hands interacting with the talisman.
3. **Signature Floating / Interacting Talisman**:
   - Each archetype holds or levitates an unmistakable glowing artifact that crystallizes their political and economic ideology.
   - The talisman sits in the upper-left or upper-right of the character, layering naturally below the card's top code pip and above the card's background.
4. **Bilateral Theme-Color Rim Lighting**:
   - Key light reflects the archetype’s exact hex token (e.g., `#FF2A54` for Market Rebel, `#8B5CF6` for Sovereign Entrepreneur).
   - Secondary edge lighting uses neutral daylight silver, warm amber, or deep obsidian shadow to provide three-dimensional depth on dark mode cards.

---

## 2. Technical Production Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspect Ratio** | `3:4` | Perfect fit for mobile and desktop card viewports |
| **Resolution** | `896 × 1200 px` (or `900 × 1200 px`) | High-DPI retina crispness on 3D card cylinder |
| **File Format** | `PNG-24` with Alpha Transparency | Seamless placement over dynamic CSS backlights |
| **Generation Base** | Pure solid black background (`#000000`) | Enables clean edge-detection and flood-fill alpha masking |
| **Subject Isolation** | Zero background scenery, zero floor shadows, zero borders | Clean character asset without container artifacts |

## 4. Extraction & Asset Preparation Pipeline

To convert the generated concepts into production-ready web assets:

1. **Generation**: Render via `generate_image` tool using the prompt formulas above on pure black (`#000000`) background at `3:4` aspect ratio.
2. **Alpha Masking (Background Removal)**:
   - Run boundary-seeded flood fill algorithm targeting background pixels (`max(R, G, B) < 22`).
   - Fill internal gaps under raised arms or between torso/cloak.
   - Apply gentle 1px alpha feathering along the boundary to avoid dark fringes.
3. **Card Integration**:
   - Save directly to `/public/archetypes/{archetype-id}.png`.
   - The card component in `IntroView.tsx` will automatically render the new PNG with its dynamic theme-colored backlight aura and 3D cylinder rotation.
